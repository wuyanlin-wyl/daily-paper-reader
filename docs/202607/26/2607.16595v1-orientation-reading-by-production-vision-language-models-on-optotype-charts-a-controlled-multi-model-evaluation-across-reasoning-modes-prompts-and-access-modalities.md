## 摘要
目的：视觉语言模型越来越多地通过消费者聊天界面用于解释医学和日常图像，但它们在实际使用的表面上辨认方向（即由E字视力表测试的单一感知操作）的能力尚未得到充分表征。方法：我们通过消费者聊天界面，在一组固定的七张视力表上评估了四个生产级视觉语言模型（分别称为Claude、GPT、GROK和Gemini）：四张均匀的E字视力表（每个基本方向一张），两张混合方向的E字视力表，以及一张Snellen字母视力表作为特异性对照。每个模型在两种推理模式（快速和思考）下运行，采用两种提示变体（有或没有明确的方向解码规则），由最多三名操作员执行。数据集包含920次可评分试验和50,420个字形判断。主要结果是相对于视力表设计方向的字形级准确率，以Wilson 95%置信区间总结。结果：在相同的视力表上，不同模型的准确率从43.0%到97.0%不等，最强模型取决于推理模式（快速模式下GPT为97.0%；思考模式下GROK为96.6%）。错误并非随机，而是汇聚到模型特定的吸引子方向。模型的内部自一致性为96-100%，但准确率范围广泛，将可靠性从有效性中分离出来。无答案键的集成共识估计与准确率高度相关（r = 0.998）。对于其中一个模型，消费者界面的准确率比程序化访问低25-27个百分点，几乎完全集中在单一方向上。结论：单一的准确率数字掩盖了临床相关的、方向特定的故障模式；在信任图像解释输出之前，应该沿多个轴并在部署表面上对视觉语言模型进行评估。

## Abstract
OBJECTIVES: Vision-language models are increasingly used to interpret medical and everyday images through consumer chat interfaces, yet their ability to read orientation - the single perceptual operation tested by the tumbling-E acuity optotype - is poorly characterized on the surfaces through which they are actually used. METHODS: We evaluated four production vision-language models (referred to as Claude, GPT, GROK, and Gemini) through their consumer chat interfaces on a locked set of seven optotype charts: four uniform tumbling-E charts (one per cardinal orientation), two mixed-orientation tumbling-E charts, and one Snellen letter chart as a specificity control. Each model was run in two reasoning modes (Fast and Thinking) under two prompt variants (with and without an explicit orientation-decoding rule) by up to three operators. The corpus comprised 920 scoreable trials and 50,420 glyph judgements. The primary outcome was glyph-level accuracy against the chart's designed orientation, summarized with Wilson 95% confidence intervals. RESULTS: Accuracy ranged from 43.0% to 97.0% across models on identical charts, and the strongest model depended on reasoning mode (GPT 97.0% in Fast mode; GROK 96.6% in Thinking mode). Errors were not random but collapsed onto a model-specific attractor direction. Models were 96-100% internally self-consistent yet ranged widely in accuracy, dissociating reliability from validity. An answer-key-free ensemble-consensus estimate tracked accuracy closely (r = 0.998). For one model, consumer-interface accuracy fell 25-27 points below programmatic access, almost entirely on a single orientation. CONCLUSIONS: A single accuracy figure conceals clinically relevant, orientation-specific failure modes; vision-language models should be evaluated along multiple axes and on the deployment surface before image-interpretation outputs are trusted.
