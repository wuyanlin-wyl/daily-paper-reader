## 摘要
多肿瘤分割对癌症早期检测很重要，并使放射科医生能够可视化、验证和理解AI预测。然而，肿瘤分割掩膜成本高昂、耗时，并且在公开数据中许多肿瘤类型都不可用。相反，医院拥有大量且易于获取的数据可用于指导分割：放射学报告、纵向图像和多期图像。我们利用这些易于获取的数据来替代肿瘤掩膜，训练AI进行肿瘤分割。为此，我们提出一种新架构RT-Super。它有一个教师网络，分析患者的纵向图像和报告，以生成高质量的肿瘤掩膜。这些掩膜用于训练一个学生网络，该学生网络只查看单张图像，不使用报告。在推理时，当纵向图像和报告不可用时，我们使用学生网络。RT-Super采用新的CNN-Transformer架构和新颖的一致性损失，利用纵向图像间肿瘤位置的一致性。我们训练RT-Super分割食管、子宫和脾脏肿瘤，这些肿瘤几乎没有或没有公开掩膜。即使没有训练掩膜，RT-Super也能分割这些肿瘤，并超越公开的AI模型。总之，我们证明，从纵向图像、多期图像和报告中学习可以克服掩膜稀缺问题，并推进多癌种检测与分割。代码：https://github.com/MrGiovanni/RT-Super

## Abstract
Multi-tumor segmentation is important for early cancer detection and allows radiologists to visualize, verify, and understand AI predictions. However, tumor segmentation masks are expensive, time-consuming, and unavailable for many tumor types in public data. Instead, hospitals have vast, readily available data that can guide segmentation: radiology reports, longitudinal images, and multi-phase images. We use this readily available data to substitute for tumor masks in training AI for tumor segmentation. To this end, we propose a new architecture, RT-Super. It has a teacher network, which analyzes the patient's longitudinal images and reports to create high-quality tumor masks. These masks train a student network, which sees a single image and no report. At inference, when longitudinal images and reports are unavailable, we use the student. RT-Super uses a new CNN-Transformer architecture and novel Consistency Losses that exploit tumor location consistency across longitudinal images. We train RT-Super to segment esophagus, uterus and spleen tumors, which have few or no public masks. Even without training masks, RT-Super can segment these tumors and surpass public AI models. Overall, we demonstrate that learning from longitudinal images, multi-phase images, and reports can overcome mask scarcity and advance multi-cancer detection and segmentation.
